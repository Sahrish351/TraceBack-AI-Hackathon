# TraceBack AI

**Evidence-first supplier invoice investigation agent.** TraceBack AI compares purchase orders, invoice lines, delivery notes, and contract prices; flags discrepancies; calculates potential exposure deterministically; and explains findings with source evidence for human review.

> **Status:** Hackathon MVP in progress. Integration status must be stated honestly. Do not label Zetaris or Meterless as working until the real end-to-end smoke test succeeds.

## Problem
Supplier invoice records can be fragmented across systems. TraceBack AI helps a reviewer find quantity/price mismatches, possible duplicates, unexpected fees, and missing evidence without treating an AI guess as a financial fact.

## Core workflow
1. Retrieve related records through the configured Zetaris integration.
2. Run deterministic reconciliation rules.
3. Coordinate the investigation/explanation workflow using the documented Meterless integration.
4. Validate the explanation against source evidence and calculation results.
5. Present a human-reviewable finding, audit trail, and supplier clarification draft.

## Safety and limitations
- Uses fictional sample data in public demos.
- Does not diagnose fraud, make payments, or send supplier emails automatically.
- Missing evidence is reported as uncertainty, not filled with guesses.
- All displayed discrepancy amounts are generated by deterministic code.
- Any model-generated explanation is constrained by source evidence and validated server-side.
